THE JOURNEY

Creating Beautiful Brides Since 2010

Welcome to Ishika Professional Salon & Spa, Ghatsila's premier destination for luxury beauty services. Founded over 14 years ago, our salon has built a reputation for excellence, creativity, and unparalleled customer service.

Our team of certified professionals is passionate about beauty. We specialize in HD and airbrush bridal makeup, creative hair styling, and rejuvenating spa therapies. We believe that every client is unique, and we tailor our services to highlight your individual features and personal style.

We pride ourselves on using only the finest international products, ensuring that you not only look stunning but that your skin and hair are nourished and protected.
